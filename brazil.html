Welcome to Brazil

Brazil is the largest country in South America. It is famous for its colorful culture, beautiful beaches, the Amazon Rainforest, football, and the Rio Carnival.

Top Attractions

Rio de Janeiro

One of Brazil's most famous cities, known for Copacabana Beach and Sugarloaf Mountain.

Christ the Redeemer

A world-famous statue overlooking Rio de Janeiro and one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

Amazon Rainforest

The largest tropical rainforest on Earth, home to incredible wildlife and biodiversity.

Famous Brazilian Food

Feijoada

Brazil's national dish made with black beans, pork, and beef.

Picanha

A popular Brazilian grilled beef steak served in many traditional restaurants.

Brigadeiro

A delicious chocolate dessert loved by people all over Brazil.
