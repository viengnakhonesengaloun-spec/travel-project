Welcome to Laos

Laos is a peaceful country famous for Buddhist temples, mountains, waterfalls and the beautiful Mekong River.

Top Attractions

Luang Prabang

UNESCO World Heritage city with beautiful temples.

Patuxai

The famous Victory Monument in Vientiane.

Kuang Si Waterfall

One of the most beautiful waterfalls in Laos.

Famous Lao Food

Larb

Traditional minced meat salad of Laos.

Kaeng Nor Mai

TA traditional Lao bamboo shoot soup cooked with herbs and yanang leaves.

Tam Mak Hoong

Spicy green papaya salad.
