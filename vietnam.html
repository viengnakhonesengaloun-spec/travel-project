Welcome to Vietnam

Vietnam is one of the most beautiful countries in Southeast Asia. It is famous for breathtaking landscapes, delicious food, friendly people, and a rich cultural heritage.

Top Attractions

Ha Long Bay

A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its thousands of limestone islands and emerald waters.

Hanoi

The capital city with thousands of years of history, temples, lakes, and delicious street food.

Da Nang

A modern coastal city famous for My Khe Beach, Dragon Bridge, and Ba Na Hills.

Hoi An Ancient Town

A charming UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its lantern streets, ancient houses, and rich history.

Sapa

A mountain town famous for breathtaking rice terraces, cool weather, and colorful ethnic cultures.

Ho Chi Minh City

Vietnam's largest city, famous for Ben Thanh Market, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and vibrant nightlife.

Famous Vietnamese Food

Pho

Vietnam's most famous noodle soup enjoyed around the world.

Banh Mi

A crispy baguette filled with meat, vegetables, and delicious sauces.

Spring Roll

Fresh rice paper rolls with shrimp, vegetables, and herbs.
