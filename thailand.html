Welcome to Thailand

Thailand is famous for its beautiful beaches, ancient temples, delicious street food, and warm hospitality. It is one of the most popular tourist destinations in Southeast Asia.

Top Attractions

Bangkok

Thailand's capital city with shopping malls, temples, and exciting nightlife.

Phuket

A world-famous island with crystal-clear beaches, resorts, and water sports.

Chiang Mai

A peaceful city in northern Thailand known for temples and mountain scenery.

Famous Thai Food

Tom Yum Goong

A spicy and sour soup with shrimp, one of Thailand's signature dishes.

Pad Thai

Stir-fried rice noodles with shrimp, peanuts, eggs, and bean sprouts.

Mango Sticky Rice

Sweet sticky rice served with fresh mango and coconut milk.
