Welcome to South Korea

South Korea is well known for its modern cities, beautiful palaces, K-Pop culture, delicious food, and breathtaking natural scenery. It is one of the most popular travel destinations in Asia.

Top Attractions

Seoul

The capital city of South Korea, famous for shopping, palaces, and modern architecture.

Gyeongbokgung Palace

The largest royal palace in Korea with beautiful traditional architecture.

N Seoul Tower

A famous landmark offering panoramic views of the entire city of Seoul.

Famous Korean Food

Kimchi

Korea's traditional fermented vegetable dish, served with almost every meal.

Bibimbap

Rice mixed with vegetables, beef, egg, and spicy chili sauce.

Korean BBQ

Delicious grilled meat enjoyed with fresh vegetables and dipping sauces.
