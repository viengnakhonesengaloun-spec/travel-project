Welcome to Japan

Japan is a fascinating country where ancient traditions meet modern technology. It is famous for cherry blossoms, temples, Mount Fuji, anime, and delicious cuisine.

Top Attractions

Mount Fuji

Japan's highest mountain and one of the country's most iconic landmarks.

Tokyo

A modern city filled with skyscrapers, shopping, entertainment, and culture.

Kyoto

Famous for beautiful temples, shrines, bamboo forests, and traditional Japanese culture.

Famous Japanese Food

Sushi

Fresh seafood served with seasoned rice, Japan's most famous dish.

Ramen

Delicious noodle soup with rich broth, meat, and vegetables.

Tempura

Crispy battered shrimp and vegetables, one of Japan's favorite foods.
